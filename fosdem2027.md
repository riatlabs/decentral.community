# Call for Participation
The Internet landscape is ever more firmly on its course towards
surveillance and centralization. A handful of cloud and CDN providers
carry most of the traffic; and take large parts of the Internet down
with them when they fail. States are building their own splinternets:
Russia blocks WhatsApp and pushes its state messenger MAX, Iran
imposed the longest nationwide shutdown on record in January 2026. The
EU's Chat Control keeps coming back, with client-side scanning and
detection orders threatening end-to-end encryption.\
Age-verification laws are turning everyday services into identity
checkpoints, Google is moving to require registration even for
sideloaded Android apps, and the AI industry treats the open web as
raw material.\
In this scenario, where most Internet-connected devices have become
tools of imprisonment rather than liberation, reviving the old
Internet ethos of peer-to-peer (P2P) and private communication is of
the utmost importance.

Taking a modest but nonetheless important step, we hereby come to announce the
return of the Decentralized Internet and Privacy Devroom at [FOSDEM 2027](https://fosdem.org/2027/).\
This devroom welcomes talks from all technical projects and initiatives which
foster decentralization and privacy on the Internet, or try to replace it for
something new altogether. We also welcome those working on securing the
devices, operating systems and user-facing software we use to access The Net.

For those new to it, FOSDEM is an annual conference on free and open source
software, attended by over 8000 developers and enthusiasts from all over the
world. It takes place in Brussels (Belgium) at the [ULB (Campus
Solbosch)](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1632534522). It is a
community-driven event and entry is free of charge.\
Beyond the main programme, FOSDEM supports dozens of devrooms, mini
conferences-within-a-conference, dedicated to discussing a specific theme.

FOSDEM 2027 takes place on the weekend of 30 and 31 January 2027. The
Decentralized Internet and Privacy Devroom will run on Sunday (31st)
for half a day (5 hours).

## Scope of Talks
We are particularly interested in work that responds to the current
wave of identity mandates, platform lockdowns, and infrastructure
consolidation - and in projects that offer practical alternatives.

Presentation topics may include:
- ad-hoc and mesh networks
- community run ISPs and networks
- anonymous overlay networks
- censorship circumvention and shutdown-resistant connectivity
- cryptography-based network stacks and protocols (e.g. public key as identity)
- post-quantum migration for private communication

- decentralized/federated network protocols and platforms
- decentralized instant messaging, video/file sharing and content storage
- self-hosting and community-hosting solutions
- resilient infrastructure: DNS, CDN, and cloud diversity
- anti-scraping, bot management, and the open web

- minimalistic and privacy-preserving browsers and extensions
- privacy-oriented operating systems
- app distribution, sideloading, and device attestation

- anonymous credentials and privacy-preserving age verification
- measurement studies of censorship, centralization, and surveillance

## Format
Talks are expected to be technical and no more than 20-25 min long. Total time
with Q&A and moderation should stay under 30 min.\
Speakers may submit multiple talks.

## Requirements
All talks should be held in English.\
Talks as well as Q&As will be recorded and later published in the FOSDEM
website. They may be also livestreamed. By hosting a talk, you agree to the
publication of its recordings, slides and other content under the [CC BY 2.0 BE
license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/be/deed.en).

While attending FOSDEM, you are required to adhere to the [FOSDEM Code of
Conduct](https://fosdem.org/2027/practical/conduct/).

## Submission
[https://fosdem.org/submit](https://fosdem.org/submit)

Talks should be submitted on the [FOSDEM pretalx](https://pretalx.fosdem.org/fosdem-2027/cfp)
making sure to select "Decentralized Internet and Privacy" in the Track
dropdown.

Use the submission notes to let us know about links to code, slides or other
resources for the talk.

## Key dates
- Submission deadline: December 2 2026, 23:59 CET
- Announcement of selected talks: December 7 2026
- Devroom day: January 31 2027 (half day)
